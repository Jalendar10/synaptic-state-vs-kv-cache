# Contributions

None of these depends on the toy probes.

| # | Contribution | Where in `main.tex` | Type |
|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | **Unified formalisation.** One associative-memory operator (state, write, read, forget, consolidate, workspace) covers softmax attention, linear attention/SSMs (RetNet, GLA, Mamba-2, DeltaNet, Titans), BDH-GPU, graph-form BDH, BDH-CQ, and CDLR. | C2 | **Retention–interference coupling (Proposition 1).** In a single state with data-independent decay, the retention of an old task and its interference with a new one are the same quantity λ^Δ. No λ gives both fast adaptation and long retention. The proposition also discusses when gating, delta rules, or sparse keys weaken this. | §3.1 | Theory |
| C3 | **CDLR architecture class.** Fast episodic Hebbian/delta state S, a slow gated consolidated bank C with merge/allocate consolidation, a halting latent workspace, a local three-factor readout adapter, and an audit log with a parameter hash. Comes with four falsifiable hypotheses (H1 capacity, H2 dual timescale, H3 workspace, H4 where synaptic memory should *lose*). | §4, Fig. 1 | Architecture |
| C4 | **Critical audit of BDH and BDH-CQ.** Every claim is tied to a section or table, with (P) paper-stated limits kept apart from (I) inferred gaps. | C5 | **Matched-compute evaluation protocol.** Matched params, tokens, and FLOPs; equal sweep budgets; modern baselines (Transformer++, Mamba/Mamba-2, GLA/Gated DeltaNet, linear attention, BDH-GPU); accuracy × memory × latency Pareto reporting; preregistration with negative results as first-class. Five tracks: E1 LM bakeoff beyond Europarl, E2 associative recall/capacity/length generalisation (tests Claim 7), E3 ablation ladder isolating Hebbian and dual-timescale effects from plain linear attention, E4 continual streams, E5 ARC-style reasoning with BDH-CQ only as a black box or a labelled reimplementation of Eqs. 1–4. Compute envelope ≈0.55–1.1k A100-hours. | §5, App. B | Methodology |
